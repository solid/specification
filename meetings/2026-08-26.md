### ODI Solid Tooling Research - Summary Findings

https://solidproject.solidcommunity.net/2026/Tooling%20research%20-%20summary%20findings.pdf

* eP: Last week we commited to reading full results and discussing them this week
* eP: 18 responses with multiple years of Solid, so a little lacking on the "fresh blood" or getting to know Solid
* ...: wonder if people who are familiar imagine people coming to Solid, or were they speaking from their experience
* PW: What was the selection process? Maybe no others are using it, so the results reflect the population
* eP: maybe we need to look for different channels, to get a better representation
* PW: feels like issues from 2020, that have been there since then; finding out what is happening in the space. Key is the commercial pod provider issue. Seems as though the whole Solid ecosystem relies on the provision of Pods.
* ...: using AI hides a lot of the issues; the audience maybe isn't there to seek; the issues are good points for this group
* EB: interesting that AI agents do the coding; questions have changed, and the specification is critical so AI can adhere to it
* PW: pointing to the spec was how he started with agents making things, and hasn't really thought about it; tests are where agents aren't as trusted
* eP: testing client can come from a taskforce or LWS WG; how easy can you expect something to be? Maybe there are complexities that we can't avoid, and provide more guidance (primers, etc.)
* LD: From the adoption stand point, it's hard as a developer. Creating someone to create pod is very unlike what people are used to. When I explain flow feels very unlike the current norm. The knowledge transfer to both developer and end user is a channange. There is not a single place to create an account. The friction I get is from the nature of Solid itself. It would be good to direct them in a single place.
* eP: good point; potential improvements: custom domains, curated directory of providers; reduce the criteria, but it is a decision requiring responsible thought; switching cost should be minimized
* PW: can someone speak as the Solid 2026 relationship to these results
* RB: these results were wrapped into the efforts
* eP: results were delayed, so it doesn't necessarily represent the latest state, but we should track and address the issues brought up; if not, how do we prioritize?
* PW: confused on the efforts for Solid 2026 having an outcome on where priorities would be
* JW: there were release issues, but there is work being done on the ODI side to address the challenges; there is a packet on auth: https://github.com/solid-contrib/reactive-authentication
* ...: implements a design pattern that has been used by other long-term Solid practitioners; as far as object models, there is a wrapper package: https://github.com/rdfjs/wrapper
* ...: on ontology and schema discovery, there has been work, but it has gone quiet; could use efforts there or at least analysis of whether to continue
* ...: server consistency is as much a spec challenge versus a tooling issue; same for fragmentation; additional https://github.com/solid/object
* eP: spec, but also an issue with the status quo despite there being better solutions; LWS is a different situation because they have to publish a spec; lots of discussion between expectations between LWS and Solid and WG v. CG
