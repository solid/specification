### Enforcing data integritiy specific to a business/knowledge domain

* eP: While generic server / smart clients has many benefits. I see gap when it comes to enforcing data integrity based on requirements of a specific business/knowledge domain. For example, booking can't be created without a vacant room, attendence can't be confirmed without a vaccination proof etc.
* ...: None of those are generic and depend on details of specific domains, can even differ based on where the domain is being put in practice (law, culture etc.)
* ...: Shapes provide very minimal validation, but often rules that require consistency across many resources are needed.
* ...: Applicable domain rules have to be defined somewhere and verified by something.
* ...: Possibly besides smart apps, also smart services are needed which ensure integrity on behalf or resource owner/controller. All requests could result in Accepted 202 and integrity service would process them.
* ...: Another option would be to have layer in front of generic storage that would provide hypermedia driven api with integrity knowledge and only allowing valid state transitions
