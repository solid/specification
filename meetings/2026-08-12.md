Solid CG is more about the exploration of the technologies, or the pieces of tech, which are necessary to fulfill the vision. Linked Web Storage (LWS) is about where the data gets written and stored, and how various applications can work with that data in various locations, and what the servers need to provide in functionality to get the data to the client applications (which are not only the "clients" that we interact with). So, I cannot personally imagine not being involved in both the WG and CG.
